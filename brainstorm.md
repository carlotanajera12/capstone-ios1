# Activity 1: App Idea Brainstorming

## Step 1: New App Ideas (at least 6)

1. **College Success Toolkit**
   A multi‑tool app for college students with daily motivation, a simple task list, and quick links to UNCC academic, wellness, and support resources.

2. **StudyBuddy Planner**
   An assignment and exam tracker with simple study session planning (e.g., “study blocks”) and light reminders.

3. **UNCC Resource Navigator**
   A directory of UNCC offices and services (UCAE, CAPS, Career Center, etc.) with descriptions, contact info, and links, plus a “favorites” section.

4. **FocusFlow Timer**
   A Pomodoro‑style study timer with customizable intervals, session history, and light stats (e.g., total focused minutes today/this week).

5. **First‑Year Survival Guide**
   A guide app for first‑year students with tips, checklists (move‑in, midterms, finals), and curated links to key campus resources.

6. **Habit Steps**
   A tiny habit tracker focused on academic habits (attend class, review notes, start assignments early) with streaks and weekly reflections.

7. **Check‑In Journal**
   A quick daily check‑in app where students log how they’re feeling, what they’re working on, and one small win for the day.


## Step 2.1: Top Two Ideas

### **Top Two App Ideas**
1. **College Success Toolkit**  
   A multi‑tool app designed to help college students stay motivated, organized, and connected to essential UNCC resources.

2. **First‑Year Survival Guide**  
   A support app for new students that includes checklists, tips, and curated campus information to help them transition into college life.



## Step 2.2: Evaluation of Top Two Ideas

### Idea 1: College Success Toolkit — Evaluation

**Mobile**  
- Uses mobile‑native features such as tab navigation, local storage, and API calls.  
- Quick access to campus resources and daily motivation is uniquely mobile-friendly.

**Story**  
- Strong story: helps UNCC students stay motivated, organized, and aware of support services.  
- Clear value for mentees, first‑years, and peers.

**Market**  
- Well‑defined niche: college students, especially those needing academic support.  
- High relevance for UNCC’s student population.

**Habit**  
- Students would open it daily for quotes, tasks, and resource checks.  
- Encourages consistent use through motivation + organization.

**Scope**  
- Very achievable within the course timeline.  
- MVP is simple (quotes API, task list, resource list).  
- Stretch goals optional and not required for success.


---

### Idea 2: First‑Year Survival Guide — Evaluation

**Mobile**  
- Uses mobile features like navigation, checklists, and local storage.  
- Could include location‑based campus info, but not required.

**Story**  
- Strong story: supports first‑year students transitioning into college life.  
- Clear value for mentees and new students.

**Market**  
- Niche but meaningful: first‑year students at UNCC or similar campuses.  
- Useful during orientation, move‑in, and first semester.

**Habit**  
- Moderate habit potential: students may use it weekly, not daily.  
- More of a reference tool than a daily-use app.

**Scope**  
- Achievable, but requires more content creation (tips, checklists, guides).  
- Less technical challenge, more content-heavy.


## Step 2.3: Final Decision

After evaluating both top ideas using the criteria (Mobile, Story, Market, Habit, Scope), the final app idea I have chosen for my capstone project is:

# **Final App Choice: College Success Toolkit**

**Reasoning:**  
- It has a strong, clear story and provides real value to UNCC students, especially mentees and first‑years.  
- It is habit‑forming, since students can use it daily for motivation, tasks, and quick access to resources.  
- The scope is realistic and achievable within the project timeline while still meeting all technical requirements (API, TableView, TabBar, Navigation, UserDefaults).  
- It offers a balanced mix of usefulness, feasibility, and impact, making it the best choice for this capstone project.
